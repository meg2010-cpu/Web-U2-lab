How to Play

Welcome to Coffee Clicker! Make as many drinks as possible before your 4-minute shift ends!

1. Start Your Shift

Click Play to begin your 4-minute shift with your starting ingredients and money.

2. Read the Order

Check the recipe name and ingredients listed on your order ticket.

3. Make the Drink

Click the ingredient images that match the recipe. Make sure you select every required ingredient!

4. Send the Order

Once you've selected all the required ingredients, click the Send Order button to earn money.

5. Visit the Shop

Use your earnings to buy new ingredients and unlock more recipes.

Complete as many orders as you can before time runs out! Check your total earnings and completed recipes at the end of your shift.

Good luck, barista!
